Patch Notes V0.1

An update on work I've completed across my website, and information about what comes next.

In the spirit of teaching my students to document changes and iterations, welcome to the first "patch notes" for my website! Starting at version 0.1 seems reasonable, mostly because I can envision more than enough notable elements to add that writing 8 more update posts before we reach version 1.0. As this is the first update, there's more here than simply changes, and if you choose to read on you'll learn about why this site exists and what I hope to achieve with it. 


Background

I was 11 or 12 when I started learning about how to make websites. My dad came home from work one day and gave me a 3 ring binder with hundreds of pages that taught the basics of HTML, Javascript, CSS, and more. I created my first webpage using Notepad, and then created perhaps 2 dozen more pages to link together. All of this was local, as the computer I used at the time wasn't even hooked up to the internet. In a further crazy twist, this was before I learned about copying and pasting, meaning that I typed every single character myself. 


Thankfully, I learned how to use keyboard shortcuts, and much more besides. My pre-teen self would be blown away by how well VS Code + Github + Cloudflare work for building and updating websites. Agentic coding has been even more important and impressive, and it's been a sort of Rosetta Stone that has helped to translate my ideas to the web.
