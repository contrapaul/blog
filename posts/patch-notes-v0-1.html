Patch Notes V0.1

An update on work I've completed across my website, and information about what comes next.

In the spirit of teaching my students to document changes and iterations, welcome to the first "patch notes" for my website! Starting at version 0.1 seems reasonable, mostly because I can envision more than enough notable elements to add that writing 8 more update posts before we reach version 1.0. As this is the first update, there's more here than simply changes, and if you choose to read on you'll learn about why this site exists and what I hope to achieve with it. 

Background

I was 11 or 12 when I started learning about how to make websites. My dad came home from work one day and gave me a 3 ring binder with hundreds of pages that taught the basics of HTML, Javascript, CSS, and more. I created my first webpage using Notepad, and then created perhaps 2 dozen more pages to link together. All of this was local, as the computer I used at the time wasn't even hooked up to the internet. In a further crazy twist, this was before I learned about copying and pasting, meaning that I typed every single character myself. 

Thankfully, I learned how to use keyboard shortcuts, and much more besides. My pre-teen self would be blown away by how well VS Code + Github + Cloudflare work for building and updating websites. Agentic coding has been even more important and impressive, and it's been a sort of Rosetta Stone that has helped to translate my ideas to the web. 

Blog/

The blog will be the least exciting portion of my site, and it's effectively done. I've prepared a reasonable efficient posting method, and will perhaps add a feature or two in the future. You'll find occasional posts about teaching, the website, end of year professional portfolio notes, and possibly thoughts on projects. 

Edu/

Edu is the home for content centered around teaching, learning, and school. The curriculum portion of the site includes a web textbook for DP Design Technology and units for MYP Design. Tools includes a range of pages that support learning, curiosity, or assessments. I intentionally placed 'fun' elements around the site, with the goal of encouraging exploration. The 'Mr. K Facts Generator' on the about page and the many site themes are examples of this. 

DP has been a major focus of development over the past few months. I started with the plan of producing study guides for each topic, and have expanded substantially to build an alternative textbook. Much remains to be done, including a huge amount of images to add, references to update, and ultimately full audio for each section. The DP specific tools also need more of the same, but they're well on their way- and notably, all of it can be used today. It only gets better in the future. 

I have more to do in MYP. Grade 6's LEGO unit is mostly built, as is grade 10's Studio Project. The remaining units are in a 'partially' to 'barely' built state at present. All will be completed within this semester, but priority is on grades 9 and 10. 

The tools portion of the site ranges from barely built to complete and solid pages. As I build out the rest of the MYP units I'll be adding more tools which help facilitate learning and assessment. 

Make/

"Make" is my generally non-school portfolio. The subdomain currently hosts a timeline of personal projects, a personal tool to help me learn Chinese, and several placeholders- as well as an extensive Blood Bowl companion site. Over time I'll add more current projects to the site, as well as student work. I'm also detailing 'Flashstone' below, which isn't located in the same directory, but the project did begin here.

Blood Bowl Companion

I've built a comprehensive companion for the tabletop game Blood Bowl. It began as a way to view a specific team roster online, and evolved considerably. At present it allows users to make accounts, save data online, play games with others across different devices, run tournaments, upload photos, and much, much more. UI updates are coming, but the site does work well. 

Flashstone

This is an in-progress hearthstone clone that lets users upload flash card decks to build their own cards and play the game while using those cards. It's a novel way to keep the flashcards in front of you, and pretty fun as well. There's a lot to do in terms of building decks, streamlining the gameplay, and adding multiplayer, but it is playable at present.
