Patch Notes v0.2

Another round of website updates

In the past few weeks I've been busy moving to a new classroom, preparing for the start of school, and now we're 3 days into the school year. At the same time I've continued to work on the website, and have made several notable updates and new tools. 

time.

Time is the first new project worth exploring. It's a schedule/timetable site geared for teachers and students. I wanted something that combines 2-week timetables shared to all teachers and students at our school with the ability to add events, remove days, and swipe through the entire year. The result is a minimal calendar that has a few special features. Users can share links to their calendar and overlay another user's calendar to look for time that doesn't overlap. (This feature is a little janky at the moment, should be cleaned up in v0.3. Another 'fun' feature is a line placed at the current time. Events that pass through the line turn gray, so you can see what has happened, and how much of something is left. The other reason I wanted to build this is that the scheduling tool used by school uses tables which are equal size for all entries- whether it's a 10 minute homeroom or 60 minute class. I wanted to have a schedule that shows a much more accurate visual representation of how long an event will take. 

cloud.

Another fun project is super lightweight word cloud tool. I built this for a staff orientation presentation when a tool that functioned exactly as requested couldn't be found. (At least, not for free). More than 140 users were active in one word cloud on the site at once during orientation, and people seemed to have a great time using it. I was able to build it in just a few hours thanks to reusing a lot of code from another recent project- "blurt."

blurt.

Blurt is directly based on Scattergories, though I haven't read the rules for Scattergories or attempted to copy anything directly. Any identical categories or features are purely coincidental. The game can be played by 12 people at a time currently, set purely as a 'reasonable number'. After playing it during homeroom at school we learned that player count should be higher, or set by the host. Players received a set of 10 categories and a letter, and must do their best to provide something from each category that starts with that letter. When the timer runs out their digital 'cards' are swapped with another player who will judge their answers and score them. I need to tweak categories and perhaps generate a list of possible words for different combinations to identify categories which shouldn't be used with specific letters.
