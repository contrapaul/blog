Patch Notes v0.3

4 weeks of school down, and many things updated. Preparing curriculum, projects, and tools for students has led to many fixes and changes, as has 'user observation' and testing. There's been more than enough added to merit a full milestone update.

make/localai

My foray into local AI has guided some changes, the most visible at the moment is the local AI site. This was produced with a combination of local and cloud coding tools, and I wanted to build a place where I could test hardware configurations and help explain 'local AI' to people. The glossary integration is a particular idea I thought up and instantly started working on for MYP and DP. 

make/tools/local

This portion of my site was built to house projects built entirely with local AI tools. At present it's a bit of a ghost town, but does have a CSS lookbook. 

/games

I started a school 'arcade' built with expanded versions of games I've enjoyed. There are several more titles planned, but I've assembled 2 competent projects that are both reasonably fun.

/edu

There are actually a lot more additions within the education site than I feel like writing about now. Interactive assessments, printable task sheets, a DP glossary, cleaned up tools and games, and much more can be found. Some notable highlights:

Electronics Parts Catalog

This page includes entries for nearly every component, microcontroller, and part in my classroom- and a lot of photos of them as well. (There are more photos to take, but maybe by time anyone reads this there will be quite a few more).

Game Mechanics Catalog

In the same vein as the electronics catalog, I've created a game mechanics page, complete with inline SVG diagrams, references to further material to research, and a set of printable sheets the classroom.


DP Review Quiz Builder

A tool to quickly build a quiz or test using the sample questions on my site. Without sharing too much- I've also 'cracked' the formula for producing high quality samples of paper 1 and 2, and will be sharing complete SL and HL practice tests soon as well. 

Flashstone

Went from prototype to a much more feature-complete prototype, with online play, a revision to focus on DP Design SL terms, four classes with hero powers, spells and weapons, gold/packs/daily quests/card backs, intro quests, and ten deck slots. So many things. 

/time

Users (me) can now export to Outlook, with some useful exporting tools.
