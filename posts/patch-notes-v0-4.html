Patch Notes v0.4

Six weeks on, and the site has moved a lot. School is in full swing, so the education pages have had the most attention, but there's been a fair amount of work across the games and tools as well. As with the last update, I've grouped things into the biggest changes rather than listing every commit, so there's almost certainly more to find than is written here.

/edu

Most of the new material is here. A whole G9 tabletop game-design unit has been built out across its phases, with task sheets, worksheets, a completion tracker, toolcards, and a production pack builder for the final one. The MYP Design Glossary now has hover definitions and a toggle for Chinese, unit pages finally print to A4 close to how they look on screen, and the assessment tools scale down to fit narrow windows. There's more added across several topic pages than I feel like itemising.

/make

Two additions. The new Soundboard is a classroom tool: record up to ten seconds onto a pad, arrange pads on a grid, set per-pad colours and fonts, and save boards to an account. And Game Bench has grown through several phases, adding tools for dice, odds, decks, session and playtest notes, rules, cards, and more.

Flashstone

Flashstone has grown from a prototype into something you can genuinely play. There's now sound and music, a logo and title screen, card art, a proper economy (gold, packs, daily quests, and card backs), a mulligan, emotes, first-match coach marks, and online play. So much has landed that I could write a page on its own.

Tome of Secrets

The most additions in one place this round. There's a meta layer now (a profile, lore, boons, seals, companions, and save codes), a "Tome of Knowledge" and an explain-on-hover glossary that walks through every rules term, accounts so a run carries over between visits, three full chapters, real enemy art and multi-layer backgrounds, and a phone layout. There's also a "help build this" page for students who want to contribute art.

/games

A new arcade title, Recess Rivals, is taking shape. There's a 14-hero roster, procedurally generated maps with a 3D viewer and an editor, and a hero sandbox for trying them out.

/trivia

The trivia night is complete: a password gate, five themed rounds (with music and video inside some of the questions), a live scoreboard, and bilingual text in Mandarin and pinyin.

New projects

build.contrapaul.com is a talk and a keepable guide, in one site, about how I actually build learning websites with AI. It has four tabs: the Talk, a Start guide to your first site, Projects, and a Glossary.

DuoShan (躲闪, "elite dodgeball") is a browser physics team dodgeball game with real-time multiplayer, ragdolls, and a server-side referee. It's in its second phase, so joining and room codes work and the feel is still being tuned.

output is an automation game for students: you drag a factory together, contracts arrive on their own, and you manage power, upgrades, and orders.

  • The cloud site picked up a proper license and notes on the third-party pieces it uses.
